Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Hidden Facts HQ and its private publishing tool, Video Factory (the "App"), handle information.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of the Hidden Facts HQ channels to upload videos to accounts the owner controls. It is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

Information the App accesses

When the owner connects their own YouTube, Instagram or Facebook account, the App receives access tokens from those services. The App uses them only to upload the owner's videos, together with titles, descriptions and tags, to the owner's own channels. The App does not read, collect or store viewer data, comments, messages, contacts or any personal information about other people.

Google user data

The App requests only the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). Data received from Google APIs is used solely to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App also uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation settings and are never published. Videos are processed temporarily and deleted after upload.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google/YouTube) or in Facebook and Instagram settings under Apps and Websites.

Viewers

If you watch our videos, your data is handled by the platform you watch on (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok) under that platform's own privacy policy. Hidden Facts HQ does not collect your data.
