=====================================================================
   CLOUD SETUP GUIDE - run the factory 24/7 for free, PC off
=====================================================================

HOW IT WORKS
  GitHub (free) runs the factory on its computers. 3 times a day it makes
  a new video and sends it to YOUR TELEGRAM with two buttons:

        [ ✅ Approve & post ]   [ ❌ Reject ]

  Tap Approve and within ~15 minutes it's posted to YouTube, Instagram
  and Facebook. For TikTok, you get the video + caption ready to post.
  Your PC can be off the whole time.

  Bot commands you can send:  /make  (new video now)   /status

  Total cost: $0. Time to set up: about 30-45 minutes, one time.
  Parts 1-3 get the factory running. Parts 4-6 are optional upgrades.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 - PUT THE FACTORY ON GITHUB  (10 min)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Go to https://github.com/signup and create a free account.
  2. Click the "+" (top right) > "New repository".
       Name: video-factory
       Choose PUBLIC. A public repository gets unlimited free run
       time, and your keys stay hidden in "Secrets", which nobody can
       read. (A private one only gets 2,000 free minutes a month,
       which isn't enough for checking approvals every 15 minutes.)
     Click "Create repository".
  3. On the new page, click the link "uploading an existing file".
  4. Open the folder  Videos\FacelessFactory-GitHub-Upload  (a clean copy
     made for this). Press Ctrl+A to select EVERYTHING inside it, and drag
     it into the GitHub page.
  5. Wait for the upload to finish, then click "Commit changes".
  6. Now add the "timer" file that makes it run 24/7:
     - In your repository click "Add file" > "Create new file".
     - In the name box type exactly:   .github/workflows/factory.yml
       (typing the "/" creates the folders automatically)
     - Open  GITHUB_WORKFLOW_factory.yml  from the upload folder with
       Notepad, press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, and paste it into the big box on GitHub.
     - Click "Commit changes".

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 - ALLOW THE FACTORY TO SAVE ITS PROGRESS  (1 min)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  In your repository: Settings > Actions > General
  Scroll to "Workflow permissions" > choose "Read and write permissions"
  > Save.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 - CREATE YOUR TELEGRAM BOT  (5 min)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Install Telegram on your phone (free) if you don't have it.
  2. In Telegram, search for  @BotFather  (blue check mark) > Start.
  3. Send:  /newbot
     Give it a name (e.g. "My Video Factory") and a username ending in
     "bot" (e.g. "myvideofactory123_bot").
  4. BotFather replies with a TOKEN that looks like 1234567:ABC-xyz...
     Keep it private.
  5. On GitHub: Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions >
     "New repository secret"
         Name:   TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN
         Secret: (paste the token)
     > Add secret.
  6. In Telegram, open your new bot and send it:  hi
  7. On GitHub: click the "Actions" tab > "factory" (left side) >
     "Run workflow" > type  poll  in the box > "Run workflow".
     Within about a minute, your bot replies: "Your chat ID is: 12345..."
  8. Add another secret:
         Name:   TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID
         Secret: (that number)
  9. TEST: Actions > factory > Run workflow > type  make  > Run.
     After about 10 minutes your first video arrives in Telegram.

  From now on it runs by itself every day.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 4 - FREE UPGRADES (strongly recommended)  (5 min)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Add these the same way (Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions):

  Secret GEMINI_API_KEY : free key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
       The AI then writes brand-new, rarely covered topics every day,
       using the viral script formula (after the 30 built-in curiosity
       scripts run out).
  Secret PEXELS_API_KEY : free key from https://www.pexels.com/api/
       Adds real stock video clips to the real photos.

  Then click the "Variables" tab (next to Secrets) > New variable:
  Variable CHANNEL_NAME    = @yourhandle      (shown in the videos)
  Variable VIDEOS_PER_RUN  = 1               (videos per run, 3 runs a day)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5 - AUTO-POST TO YOUTUBE  (15 min, done once on your PC)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Follow "STEP 1" and "STEP 4" in START HERE.txt on your PC (install,
     then create client_secret.json and run YOUTUBE_SIGNIN.bat).
  2. IMPORTANT: in Google Cloud > "Google Auth Platform" > "Audience",
     click "PUBLISH APP" (status "In production"). If you skip this,
     Google expires your sign-in after 7 days.
  3. Open  data\youtube_token.json  in Notepad, select all, copy.
  4. GitHub secret:  YOUTUBE_TOKEN_JSON  = (paste everything)
  5. Optional variable:  YOUTUBE_PRIVACY = public
     Note: until your Google project passes YouTube's free API audit,
     YouTube keeps API uploads private. Apply here:
     https://support.google.com/youtube/contact/yt_api_form
     Until then, flip each Short to Public in YouTube Studio, which
     takes one tap in the YouTube Studio app.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 6 - AUTO-POST TO INSTAGRAM + FACEBOOK  (20 min)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  You need:
    - an Instagram PROFESSIONAL account (Business or Creator, free
      to switch in Instagram settings)
    - a Facebook PAGE, linked to that Instagram account
      (Instagram > Settings > Accounts Center, or in the Page's settings)

  1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com > log in > "My Apps" >
     "Create App". Pick the use case for managing your Page and
     Instagram content (wording changes often; choose the option
     that mentions Pages / Instagram API with Facebook Login).
  2. Open https://developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer
     - Select your app (top right).
     - "Add a permission" and tick:
         pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts,
         instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, business_management
     - Click "Generate Access Token" and allow everything for your Page
       and Instagram account. Copy the token.
  3. In your app: App settings > Basic > copy the App ID and App Secret.
  4. On your PC, double-click META_SETUP.bat and paste the three values.
     It prints  META_PAGE_ID, META_PAGE_TOKEN, IG_USER_ID.
  5. Add those 3 as GitHub secrets (same names).

  Your app can stay in "Development" mode, because it only posts to your
  own accounts.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 7 - TIKTOK
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  TikTok only allows automatic public posting for apps that pass its
  developer review (it needs a website and a privacy policy). Until then,
  after you approve a video the bot sends you its TikTok caption:
    long-press the video in Telegram > Save > open TikTok > + >
    pick the video > paste the caption > Post.   (about 20 seconds)
  Tip: turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label when posting.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
GOOD TO KNOW
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Schedule: videos are made at about 6 AM, 11 AM and 4 PM (Edmonton
    time). Change the times in .github/workflows/factory.yml (line with
    "23 12,17,22"; these are UTC hours).
  - Approvals are checked every 15 minutes (GitHub sometimes runs a few
    minutes late).
  - Something wrong? Actions tab > click the red run > read the log, or
    paste it to Claude.
  - Pause everything: Actions > factory > "..." > Disable workflow.
