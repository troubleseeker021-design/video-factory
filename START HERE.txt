WHAT THIS IS
------------
A mini "factory" of AI agents that runs on your own PC, for free:

  1. Writer       picks or writes a fact script        (factory/writer.py)
  2. Narrator     turns it into an AI voice-over       (factory/voice.py)
  3. Composer     makes original background music      (factory/music.py)
  4. Designer     makes the moving background          (factory/visuals.py)
  5. Editor       adds the title, word-by-word captions,
                  progress bar and exports a 1080x1920 video
                                                       (factory/render.py)
  7. Sound FX     whooshes, pops, dings, impacts and ticks timed to
                  every cut                            (factory/sfx.py)
  8. Researcher   finds real photos for each scene from Wikipedia
                  (free, no key) or Pexels footage     (factory/media.py)
  6. Publisher    after YOU approve, uploads to YouTube and gives you
                  ready-to-paste captions for TikTok, Instagram and
                  Facebook                             (factory/dashboard.py)

Every finished video waits for your approval. Nothing is posted
without your click.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - INSTALL (one time, about 5 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click  SETUP.bat
     - If Python isn't installed, the download page opens. Install it.
       IMPORTANT: on the first installer screen, tick
       "Add python.exe to PATH". Then double-click SETUP.bat again.
  2. Wait until it says "Setup complete!"

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - PERSONALIZE (2 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Right-click config.json > Open with > Notepad. Change:
    "channel_name": "@YourChannel"   ->  your real handle
  Save. (Keep the quotes and commas exactly as they are.)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - MAKE AND APPROVE VIDEOS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click  MAKE_VIDEOS.bat
     It makes 3 videos (about 1 minute each), then opens your
     review page in the browser.
  2. On the review page, watch each video:
       Approve  -> moves it to "Approved"
       Reject   -> it's set aside and never posted
  3. In the "Approved" tab, each video has buttons:
       Show video file      opens the folder holding the video
       Open TikTok          opens TikTok's upload page. Drag the video in,
                            click "Copy" on the TikTok caption, paste, post.
       Open Instagram +     opens Meta Business Suite. One upload posts to
       Facebook             BOTH Instagram and Facebook (and can schedule).
       Mark ... posted      ticks it off. When all are ticked, the video
                            moves to "Posted".

  Want to review later? Just double-click REVIEW.bat any time.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - YOUTUBE AUTO-UPLOAD (optional, about 15 minutes, one time)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  After this, clicking Approve uploads the Short to YouTube by itself.

  1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Google
     account that owns your YouTube channel.
  2. Create a project (any name, e.g. "Video Factory").
  3. Search "YouTube Data API v3" > Enable.
  4. Go to "Google Auth Platform" (or "OAuth consent screen"):
     - User type: External. Fill in the app name and your email.
     - Under "Audience" / "Test users", add your own Gmail address.
  5. Go to Clients (or Credentials) > Create client > "Desktop app".
     Download the JSON file.
  6. Rename that file to  client_secret.json  and put it in the
     "data" folder inside this factory.
  7. Double-click YOUTUBE_SIGNIN.bat, then sign in and allow access.
     (Google warns "app not verified". That's normal, because it's your
     own app. Click Continue.)
  8. In config.json set   "youtube_upload_enabled": true

  Note: Google makes uploads from new personal API projects PRIVATE
  until the project passes a free YouTube audit. Until then, open
  YouTube Studio and switch each approved Short to Public, which takes
  one click. You can apply for the audit at
  https://support.google.com/youtube/contact/yt_api_form

---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - AUTOPILOT (make videos every day automatically)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Double-click AUTOPILOT_ON.bat
  Every day at 9:00 AM (while your PC is on) the factory makes new
  videos. You just open REVIEW.bat and approve.
  To stop: AUTOPILOT_OFF.bat

HOW TO ACTUALLY MAKE MONEY (read this)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Consistency wins: post 1-3 videos a day on every platform for at
    least 60-90 days before judging results.
  - Platforms demonetize "mass-produced / repetitive" content. Keep
    variety: rotate topics, use the Gemini + Pexels upgrades, and
    reject weak videos. Quality over quantity.
  - Turn on each platform's "AI-generated content" label when posting
    (TikTok has a toggle, and YouTube is marked automatically by this
    factory). Being honest protects your account.
  - Monetization programs and their requirements change often, so check
    the current rules for the YouTube Partner Program, TikTok Creator
    Rewards and Facebook/Instagram monetization.
  - Fastest early money is often not ad revenue: affiliate links in
    your bio (books, gadgets related to your niche) can pay sooner.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Look at output\logs\factory.log for the error message.
  - "python is not recognized": reinstall Python and tick
    "Add python.exe to PATH".
  - Voice fails: check your internet connection (the free AI voice
    needs internet).
  - Ask Claude! Paste the error and it will help you fix it.
